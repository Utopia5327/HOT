FIND YOUR WAY

Explore at your pace.

From above

Drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan. Choose Explore on foot to enter. Hover over a gallery to reveal its marker, or choose a project from the menu.

On foot

Drag to look. Use W A S D or the arrow keys to walk. Hold Shift to move faster. On touch screens, use the direction buttons.

Discover the work

Click an artwork or the nearby project title. Press E to open the nearest work. The website navigation includes every project without walking.

Let the place guide you

Find Guided walk and Reveal interiors in the menu. Escape returns to the full landscape.

Films & sound

Stand in a glowing area and face the screen to play its video. Videos pause when you leave. Enable Sound once to hear the nearest film. Audio fades with distance. Some process animations are silent.
