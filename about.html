Manas Bhatia

Computational Designer · Spatial Artist

Computational designer and spatial artist working at the intersection of generative AI, parametric systems, and architectural thinking. M.S. in Computational Design Practices, Columbia University GSAPP (2024–25). Research centred on AI-driven participatory spatial design — building tools that make computation accessible to non-experts. Practice spans Grasshopper, LLM-based design agents, GIS-scaled urban analysis, and kinetic fabrication. Work exhibited at Château de Lourmarin, France; Google UnFold gallery; covered by CNN, BBC, Dezeen, Designboom. Featured guest, PBS A Brief History of the Future, Ep. 06. Forbes 30 Under 30 nominee, 2024. Prior: Associate Architect, Ant Studio, India.
